## Tariffs, trade architecture and the mercantilist toolkit

**F1.** The US average effective tariff rate will be at or above 10% on December 31, 2027. **P = 0.80**
*Resolution: Yale Budget Lab "State of US Tariffs" (or Penn Wharton Budget Model if discontinued), average effective tariff rate on all imports, first publication after December 31, 2027.*

**F2.** By December 31, 2028, at least three G7 countries will have in force AI-specific trade measures (tariffs on AI chips, export controls on model weights, or mandatory data or compute localization for frontier AI). **P = 0.70**
*Resolution: official gazettes or trade-ministry announcements of the G7 members; measure must explicitly name AI systems, AI chips or model weights.*

**F3.** The WTO Appellate Body will still lack a functioning quorum on December 31, 2030. **P = 0.85**
*Resolution: WTO official records on Appellate Body membership as of that date.*

**F4.** A documented government-to-government agreement explicitly exchanging access to AI compute or advanced AI chips for critical minerals or energy supply will be signed by December 31, 2028. **P = 0.70**
*Resolution: primary government announcement or credible reporting (FT, Reuters, Bloomberg) describing chips-or-compute-for-resources terms in a single state-level deal.*

**F5.** A G20 government other than the United States will take a direct equity stake or golden share in a domestic frontier AI or advanced semiconductor company by December 31, 2029. **P = 0.60**
*Resolution: official announcement of state shareholding (any size) or golden-share rights in a company whose primary business is frontier AI models or sub-7nm chip design or fabrication.*

## The chip war and the compute map

**F6.** China will produce at least one million 7nm-class or better AI accelerators in a single calendar year by the end of 2028. **P = 0.60**
*Resolution: consensus of at least two credible estimates (e.g. SemiAnalysis, CSIS, TechInsights) for any calendar year through 2028.*

**F7.** Nvidia's share of global AI accelerator revenue will fall below 60% in at least one quarter before December 31, 2029. **P = 0.55**
*Resolution: quarterly datacenter accelerator revenue-share estimates from at least two of IDC, Mercury Research, TechInsights or SemiAnalysis.*

**F8.** TSMC's share of global installed leading-edge logic capacity (3nm-class and below) will fall below 60% by December 31, 2031. **P = 0.45**
*Resolution: capacity-share estimates from at least two of TrendForce, IC Insights successor datasets, SEMI or SemiAnalysis.*

**F9.** A Gulf state (Saudi Arabia or the UAE) will host one of the world's ten largest AI compute clusters by December 31, 2028. **P = 0.75**
*Resolution: Epoch AI's public cluster tracking (or TOP500 aggregate by country if Epoch discontinues), measured by installed training compute.*

**F10.** India will rank among the top three countries in installed AI training compute by December 31, 2030. **P = 0.20**
*Resolution: Epoch AI or comparable country-level installed-compute estimates as of that date.*

## The AI economy: boom, bust and diffusion

**F11.** Combined AI-infrastructure capital expenditure of the five largest US hyperscalers will decline year over year in at least one calendar year before December 31, 2029. **P = 0.55**
*Resolution: reported annual capex of Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta and Oracle from 10-K filings; YES if the five-company total falls in any year 2027, 2028 or 2029 versus the prior year.*

**F12.** US data centers will consume more than 10% of total US electricity in a calendar year by December 31, 2030. **P = 0.40**
*Resolution: EIA (or LBNL) official estimate of annual data-center share of US electricity consumption for any year through 2030.*

**F13.** A Chinese-origin open-weights model will hold the #1 position on the leading public LLM leaderboard (LMArena or successor) for 30 or more consecutive days before December 31, 2027. **P = 0.35**
*Resolution: public leaderboard history; model must have downloadable weights and a Chinese lab as primary developer.*

**F14.** US "office and administrative support" employment (BLS CES) will be at least 5% below its 2025 annual average in any calendar-year average through 2028. **P = 0.55**
*Resolution: BLS Current Employment Statistics annual averages, occupational employment for office and administrative support, 2026, 2027 or 2028 versus 2025.*

**F15.** US nonfarm labor productivity growth will average 2.5% or more over any rolling three-year window ending on or before December 31, 2031. **P = 0.40**
*Resolution: BLS quarterly labor productivity series, three-year annualized average.*

## Blocs, money and the non-aligned middle

**F16.** At least 40 countries will have published national AI strategies that explicitly invoke "sovereign AI," "sovereign compute" or national compute reserves by December 31, 2027. **P = 0.75**
*Resolution: OECD.AI policy observatory plus official strategy documents; count of countries whose published strategy uses sovereignty language for AI or compute.*

**F17.** A BRICS-linked cross-border payment or settlement system will process more than USD 100 billion in a calendar year by December 31, 2029. **P = 0.35**
*Resolution: official BRICS or member-central-bank reporting, or consensus of credible financial press estimates, for any qualifying system (e.g. BRICS Pay or an mBridge-derived system with BRICS members).*

**F18.** The US dollar's share of allocated global FX reserves will fall below 50% by December 31, 2032. **P = 0.25**
*Resolution: IMF COFER data, any quarter through 2032.*

**F19.** The US and China will sign a formal bilateral agreement on military AI or frontier AI safety by December 31, 2030. **P = 0.30**
*Resolution: signed bilateral instrument (treaty, MOU or joint declaration with named commitments) announced by both governments; broader multilateral statements do not count.*

**F20.** A low- or lower-middle-income country (World Bank classification) will operate a public-sector AI system (health, education or government services) with more than 50 million registered or active users by December 31, 2029. **P = 0.60**
*Resolution: official government reporting or two credible independent reports; system must be AI-based (not only digital ID or payments) and the country classified low or lower-middle income at announcement.*
