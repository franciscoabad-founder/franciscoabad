# Forecasting the Future 2026: submission package

Challenge: **Forecasting the Future: A Modern Economics Challenge** (Bridgewater Associates x Global Citizen).

- Theme: how Modern Mercantilism and AI reshape trade, policy and power over the next decade.
- Deadline: **August 1, 2026, 11:59 PM ET**.
- Portal: https://www.globalcitizen.org/en/programs/forecasting-the-future/ (also announced at https://observatory.bwater.com/events/2026-forecasting-the-future).
- Prize: the five highest-ranked, best-calibrated submissions receive USD 25,000 each, plus consideration for a role or internship at Bridgewater.
- Judging emphasis: calibration (probabilities that match outcomes), clarity of resolution criteria, quality of the framework and reasoning.

## Package contents

| File | Challenge component | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `01-forecasts.md` | Forecasts | Minimum 10 binary forecasts, one sentence each, probability + objective resolution criteria, 1 to 10 year horizons. This package includes 20. |
| `02-framework.md` | Framework & Holistic Synthesis | Max 3 pages. Big-picture view of how AI and Modern Mercantilism shape the next decade. |
| `03-analytical-appendix.md` | Analytical Appendix | Reasoning, evidence and analysis behind each forecast. |

## Submission checklist (before August 1)

1. Read the official 2026 terms and conditions at bridgewater.com and confirm the exact minimum number of forecasts and page limits on the form itself.
2. Paste or upload the three components in the portal exactly as structured here.
3. Verify every probability one last time against the latest data (tariff tracker, capex guidance, chip policy news).
4. Confirm personal details and eligibility (18+, open worldwide, no finance background required).
5. Submit and save the confirmation email.

Note: this folder is reference material for the application. It is not part of the website build (`apps/web/`).
